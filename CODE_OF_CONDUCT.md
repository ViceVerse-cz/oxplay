# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical choices with
evidence, welcome questions, and give constructive feedback. Disagreement is
expected; personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination and sexualized
attention are not acceptable.

Respect privacy. Do not publish someone's personal information or credentials,
pressure anyone to share an account, or encourage abuse of a platform or its
creators. These expectations apply in project discussions, reviews, issues and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation after
reviewing the behavior and context. They should explain decisions privately
where appropriate, protect reporters, and allow affected participants to seek
review by another maintainer when available.

Report concerns privately through a verified maintainer contact published by
the repository host. This initial source tree does not yet designate a private
conduct contact; maintainers must establish one before opening a public
community. Do not post sensitive incident details while requesting that contact.
